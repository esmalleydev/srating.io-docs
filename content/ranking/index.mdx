Rankings are our computed leaderboards for a whole division in a season — every team, player, transfer, conference, or coach ranked against each other, with the rank for each individual stat.

[Download a ranking](/ranking/download) pulls one down in a single request, already flattened for a spreadsheet or dataframe.

### Available rankings

| `view` | Ranks | Rows keyed by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `team` | Every team in the division | `statistic_ranking_id` |
| `player` | Every player in the division | `player_statistic_ranking_id` |
| `transfer` | Players in the transfer portal (college basketball) | `player_statistic_ranking_id` |
| `conference` | Every conference | `conference_statistic_ranking_id` |
| `coach` | Every coach | `coach_statistic_ranking_id` |

Each row carries the same stat columns you get from [team/getStats](/team/getStats) — the season averages plus a matching `_rank` for each one.
