One API for college sports data: scores, odds, teams, players, boxscores, advanced stats and predictions, all through a simple `class` / `function` shape.

### Sports covered

- **College basketball** — NCAA Division I men's basketball
- **College football** — FBS

Both sports share the same tables and endpoints. You just point requests at a different `organization_id` / `division_id` -- see [Sports](/sports). New sports get added to the same schema, so nothing about how you query changes.

### What you can pull

| Category | What's in it | Where to start |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Games & scores | Schedules, live scores, final results | [getGames](/game/getGames) |
| Odds | Money line, spread, over/under -- pregame and live | [getOdds](/game/getOdds) |
| Boxscores | Full team and player box scores for every game | [getBoxscore](/boxscore/getBoxscore), [getPlayerBoxscore](/player_boxscore/getPlayerBoxscore) |
| Team & player stats | Season averages, advanced efficiency metrics, and rank within the division | [getStats](/team/getStats), [getGameLogs](/player/getGameLogs) |
| Elo ratings | A single strength rating per team, current or for a past season | [elo](/elo/get) |
| Teams, players, coaches, conferences | Rosters, bios, and affiliations | [team](/team/get), [player](/player/get), [coach](/coach/get), [conference](/conference/get) |
| Transfer portal | Player transfer commitments by season (college basketball) | [cbb_transfer_player_season](/cbb_transfer_player_season/get) |
| Predictions | Win probability and projected score for every game, plus projected conference standings | [getSchedulePredictions](/team/getSchedulePredictions), [getConferencePrediction](/conference/getConferencePrediction) |
| Rankings | Every team, player, transfer, conference, and coach ranked for a season | [Rankings](/ranking) |
| Search | Look up a team, player, or coach by name to get its id | [search](/search) |

### Why people use it

- **It's live.** Scores and odds update in real time, not just after final.
- **The advanced stats are already computed.** Pace, offensive/defensive rating, and efficiency come back ready to use -- no deriving them yourself from raw box scores.
- **You get our model, not just the data.** Win probability and projected score for every game, and projected standings for every conference.
- **History is included.** Past seasons, box scores, and odds movement are queryable the same way as today's data, which makes it easy to backtest a model.
- **One schema, every sport.** Learn the shape once and it works the same way for basketball and football.
